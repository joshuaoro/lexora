### 1. What this study is about

We have built a web application called **LEXORA** that helps children practise
reading Filipino words one word at a time. We are asking whether it works, and
whether reading specialists find it useful and usable.

We are inviting your child to use LEXORA for about `[8]` weeks so we can find out.

### 2. What your child will do

- Use LEXORA on a tablet or computer at The Reading Owl, about `[2]` times a week
  for around `[15–20]` minutes each time.
- Read Filipino words aloud into a microphone. The application listens and tells
  the child whether the word was read correctly.
- Do short activities on rhyming, first sounds, and counting syllables.
- A few times, read some **made-up words** — these are not real Filipino words.
  This is on purpose: it shows whether a child is sounding words out rather than
  remembering them. Your child will not be told they got these wrong, and they
  do not affect anything in the app.

Your child's reading specialist will be present. Sessions happen during normal
centre hours and are not extra homework.

### 3. What information we collect

- **Account details** — your child's first name or a nickname, an email address
  for signing in, and a password stored in scrambled form that nobody can read.
- **Reading activity** — which words were shown, what the computer heard, whether
  each reading was correct, the kind of mistake, and how long each word took.
- **Voice recordings** — a short audio clip of your child reading each word
  aloud.
- **Display settings** — the font, text size and colours your child prefers.

**We do not collect** your child's address, birthday, school records, photographs
or video.

### 4. About the voice recordings — please read this part carefully

This is the most sensitive information we collect, so we want to be exact.

**Why we record.** The computer's judgement of whether a word was read correctly
can be wrong. A reading specialist listens to the recording and says whether the
computer was right. Without the recordings we could not check the computer's
work, and checking it is a main purpose of this study.

**Where they go.** To score a reading, the clip is sent to a company called
**Groq**, which runs speech-recognition software and sends back a text version of
what was said. The recordings are **not used to train or improve any
artificial-intelligence model** — not ours, and not theirs.

**Who can hear them.** The reading specialists at The Reading Owl and the
researchers. Nobody else.

**How long we keep them.** Recordings are deleted automatically after
**180 days**. A specialist can delete them sooner, and normally does as soon as
the checking is finished. When a recording is deleted, the scores and notes stay
— **no result changes**, we simply can no longer listen back.

### 5. Who can see your child's information

- Your child sees only their own records.
- Reading specialists at The Reading Owl see the records of children in the
  programme, which they need in order to plan teaching.
- The researchers see the records for the study.
- **In anything we publish or present, your child is identified only by a code**
  such as `L1`. No names appear, and no recording is ever played publicly.

### 6. Risks

We do not expect this to harm your child. The realistic risks are small:

- **Frustration or tiredness.** Reading aloud is effortful for a child with
  dyslexia. Your child may stop at any moment, and the specialist will end a
  session if the child is distressed.
- **Privacy.** Any information kept on a computer carries some risk. What we do
  about it: access needs an account and a password, the data is stored in a
  protected database, recordings are deleted after 180 days, and reports use
  codes rather than names.

### 7. Benefits

Your child may improve at reading single words, and their specialist gets a
clearer picture of which sounds and word shapes are difficult. We cannot promise
improvement. Findings may help other children with dyslexia in the future.

### 8. LEXORA does not diagnose

LEXORA is a reading **support and practice** tool. **It does not diagnose
dyslexia**, it is not a clinical assessment, and it does not replace a teacher,
reading specialist, speech-language pathologist or doctor. Nothing it reports
should be treated as a diagnosis.

### 9. Taking part is voluntary

- You do not have to agree, and your child does not have to take part.
- **You may withdraw at any time, for any reason, without giving one.**
- Your child may stop at any time even if you have agreed. We also ask the child
  directly, and we respect a "no".
- **Refusing or withdrawing will not affect the services your child receives from
  The Reading Owl in any way.**

**If you withdraw**, tell any of the contacts below and we will delete your
child's account and everything attached to it — readings, recordings, scores and
notes. This deletion cannot be undone. If you would rather we kept the readings
already collected but stopped collecting more, say so and we will do that
instead.

### 10. Your rights under the Data Privacy Act

Under the **Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)**, you
may ask to **see** the information we hold about your child, ask us to **correct**
anything wrong, ask us to **delete** it, and **object** to how it is used. To do
any of these, contact us below.

## Filipino

**Pamagat ng pag-aaral:** Disenyo at Pagbuo ng LEXORA, isang Web Application na
Tumutulong sa Pagbasa at Sumusubaybay sa Progreso para sa mga May Dyslexia

**Mga mananaliksik:** `[pangalan]`, `[kurso]`, `[paaralan]`
**Tagapayo:** `[pangalan]` · **Contact:** `[email / cellphone]`
**Katuwang na institusyon:** The Reading Owl, Davao City

---

### 1. Tungkol saan ang pag-aaral

Gumawa kami ng web application na tinatawag na **LEXORA**. Tumutulong ito sa mga
bata na magsanay sa pagbasa ng mga salitang Filipino, isa-isa. Gusto naming
malaman kung gumagana ito, at kung kapaki-pakinabang at madaling gamitin para sa
mga reading specialist.

Inaanyayahan namin ang inyong anak na gumamit ng LEXORA sa loob ng humigit-kumulang
`[8]` na linggo.

### 2. Ano ang gagawin ng inyong anak

- Gagamit ng LEXORA sa tablet o computer sa The Reading Owl, mga `[2]` beses
  kada linggo, tig-`[15–20]` minuto.
- Babasahin nang malakas ang mga salitang Filipino sa mikropono. Nakikinig ang
  app at sinasabi kung tama ang pagbasa.
- Gagawa ng maiikling gawain tungkol sa tugma, unang tunog, at bilang ng pantig.
- Ilang beses, magbabasa ng **mga imbentong salita** — hindi ito tunay na salitang
  Filipino. Sinasadya ito: ipinapakita nito kung binabaybay ba talaga ng bata ang
  salita o kabisado lang. Hindi sasabihin sa bata na mali sila rito, at wala itong
  epekto sa app.

Kasama ang reading specialist ng inyong anak. Nangyayari ito sa loob ng karaniwang
oras ng sentro at hindi ito dagdag na takdang-aralin.

### 3. Anong impormasyon ang kinokolekta namin

- **Detalye ng account** — palayaw o unang pangalan ng bata, email para
  makapag-sign in, at password na naka-scramble at hindi mababasa ninuman.
- **Aktibidad sa pagbasa** — anong salita ang lumabas, ano ang narinig ng
  computer, tama ba ang pagbasa, anong uri ng mali, at gaano katagal bawat salita.
- **Voice recording** — maikling audio ng pagbasa ng bata sa bawat salita.
- **Setting ng display** — font, laki ng titik, at kulay na gusto ng bata.

**Hindi namin kinokolekta** ang address, kaarawan, school records, litrato o video
ng inyong anak.

### 4. Tungkol sa voice recording — mahalagang basahin ito

Ito ang pinaka-sensitibong impormasyon, kaya nais naming maging tiyak.

**Bakit nagre-record.** Puwedeng magkamali ang computer sa paghusga kung tama ang
pagbasa. Pinapakinggan ng reading specialist ang recording at sinasabi kung tama
ba ang computer. Kung walang recording, hindi namin masusuri ang computer — at
ito ang isa sa pangunahing layunin ng pag-aaral.

**Saan ito napupunta.** Para masuri ang pagbasa, ipinapadala ang audio sa
kompanyang **Groq**, na nagpapatakbo ng speech-recognition software at nagbabalik
ng nakasulat na bersyon ng sinabi. **Hindi ginagamit ang mga recording para
sanayin o pagandahin ang kahit anong artificial intelligence** — hindi sa amin,
at hindi sa kanila.

**Sino ang makakarinig.** Ang mga reading specialist ng The Reading Owl at ang mga
mananaliksik. Wala nang iba.

**Gaano katagal itinatago.** Awtomatikong binubura ang recording pagkatapos ng
**180 araw**. Puwede itong burahin ng specialist nang mas maaga, at karaniwang
ginagawa ito kapag tapos na ang pagsusuri. Kapag nabura ang recording, nananatili
ang mga iskor at tala — **walang nagbabagong resulta**, hindi na lang namin
mapapakinggan muli.

### 5. Sino ang makakakita ng impormasyon ng inyong anak

- Ang inyong anak — sarili lang niyang rekord.
- Ang mga reading specialist ng The Reading Owl — para makapagplano ng turo.
- Ang mga mananaliksik — para sa pag-aaral.
- **Sa anumang ilalathala o ipapakita, code lang ang gagamitin** gaya ng `L1`.
  Walang pangalang lalabas, at walang recording na ipapatugtog sa publiko.

### 6. Mga panganib

Hindi namin inaasahang makakasama ito sa inyong anak. Maliit lang ang panganib:

- **Pagkabigo o pagod.** Mahirap ang pagbasa nang malakas para sa batang may
  dyslexia. Puwedeng huminto ang inyong anak anumang oras, at ititigil ng
  specialist ang session kung nababalisa ang bata.
- **Pribasiya.** May kaunting panganib sa anumang impormasyong nasa computer. Ang
  ginagawa namin: kailangan ng account at password, protektado ang database,
  binubura ang recording pagkatapos ng 180 araw, at code ang ginagamit sa mga
  ulat sa halip na pangalan.

### 7. Mga benepisyo

Posibleng gumaling ang inyong anak sa pagbasa ng iisang salita, at magkakaroon ang
specialist ng mas malinaw na larawan kung anong tunog at hugis ng salita ang
mahirap. **Hindi namin ito maipapangako.** Maaaring makatulong ang natuklasan sa
ibang batang may dyslexia sa hinaharap.

### 8. Hindi nagdi-diagnose ang LEXORA

Ang LEXORA ay kasangkapan para sa **pagsasanay at suporta** sa pagbasa. **Hindi
ito nagdi-diagnose ng dyslexia**, hindi ito clinical assessment, at hindi nito
pinapalitan ang guro, reading specialist, speech-language pathologist o doktor.
Walang anuman dito ang dapat ituring na diagnosis.

### 9. Boluntaryo ang paglahok

- Hindi kayo obligadong pumayag.
- **Puwede kayong umatras anumang oras, sa anumang dahilan, at hindi ninyo
  kailangang magpaliwanag.**
- Puwedeng huminto ang inyong anak kahit pumayag na kayo. Tinatanong din namin
  nang direkta ang bata, at iginagalang namin ang "ayaw".
- **Ang pagtanggi o pag-atras ay walang epekto sa serbisyong natatanggap ng
  inyong anak mula sa The Reading Owl.**

**Kung aatras kayo**, sabihin lang sa amin at buburahin namin ang account ng
inyong anak at lahat ng kalakip nito. Hindi na ito maibabalik. Kung mas gusto
ninyong itago ang nakolekta na pero itigil ang pagkolekta, sabihin lang at iyon
ang gagawin namin.

### 10. Mga karapatan ninyo sa ilalim ng Data Privacy Act

Sa ilalim ng **Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)**, puwede
ninyong hilingin na **makita** ang impormasyon tungkol sa inyong anak,
**itama** ang anumang mali, **burahin** ito, at **tutulan** ang paggamit nito.
Makipag-ugnayan lang sa amin.
